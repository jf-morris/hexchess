Hex Chess

McCooey's Hexagonal Chess. Click a piece, then a highlighted cell to move.
About Hex Chess — Hexagonal chess is a family of chess variants played on boards of hexagonal cells rather than the traditional 8×8 grid, each with its own board shape, starting array, and rulesets. Learn the rules and read more about its history on Wikipedia. Learn more about this Hex Chess project on GitHub.
